# External service marks

These marks identify outbound links to the named services. They are not Tempo branding and do not indicate sponsorship or endorsement. Brand marks remain the property of their owners.

- `chatgpt.svg`: unmodified white monoblossom from OpenAI's [official logo archive](https://cdn.openai.com/brand/OpenAI-Logos-2025.zip), distributed through [OpenAI Design Guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/).
- `perplexity.svg`: unmodified [Perplexity website favicon](https://www.perplexity.ai/favicon.svg).
- `gemini.webp`: web-size derivative of the [Google Gemini app's official icon](https://www.gstatic.com/lamda/images/gemini_sparkle_4g_512_lt_f94943af3be039176192d.png), preserving the complete artwork and colour.
- `linkedin.webp`: lossless web-size derivative of `InBug-Black.png` from [LinkedIn's official logo archive](https://content.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/xbu/linkedin-revised-brand-guidelines/logos/in-logo.zip), linked from [LinkedIn Brand Downloads](https://brand.linkedin.com/downloads). The full mark, proportions and registered trademark symbol are preserved.
- `claude.svg` and `grok.svg`: unmodified vector marks from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` version 1.98.0 (`icons/claude-color.svg` and `icons/grok.svg`). Source package license: MIT; brand/trademark rights remain with their owners. See [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal) and [Grok brand guidelines](https://x.ai/legal/brand-guidelines).

Checked 25 October 2026. Assets are local, fixed-size and lazy-loaded; no logo CDN or tracking service is called by a visitor's browser.
