Getting started

How do I get started with Tempo?

Once you have access, open Tempo and follow the one-time setup to connect the tools you use. Tempo syncs your schedule and connected apps in the background, then starts surfacing planning suggestions, briefings, and drafts. Everything is a suggestion until you approve it.

Which platforms is Tempo available on?

Right now Tempo is a native iPhone app in private beta. When we publicly release, Tempo will also be available on desktop and Android. Release and App Store timing will be announced as we get closer.

Integrations

How do I connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, or Outlook?

You connect your tools from the Updates page in Tempo. Open Updates, choose the service you'd like to connect, and sign in securely with that provider to grant Tempo the permissions it needs.

Gmail, Google Calendar, and Outlook are ready to use as soon as they're connected. Notion takes one extra step, which is covered below.

How do I set up the Notion integration?

Notion takes a couple of extra steps so you decide exactly which pages Tempo can access:

  1. Go to the Updates page and connect Notion.
  2. Open Settings and choose which Notion pages Tempo is allowed to use.
  3. Click Save.

Once you've saved your selection, the Notion integration is ready to use.

Does Tempo send emails or change my schedule without asking?

No. Tempo prepares drafts and proposed schedule changes for you to review. Nothing is sent and nothing moves until you approve it.

Beta & access

How do I join the beta and get access?

Join the waitlist on our home page. Most invites go out by email within 48 hours. If you don't see it, please check your spam or promotions folder.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

We'd love to hear it. Email tempo.calendar.ai@gmail.com with a short description. For bugs, including your device, operating system, and the steps that led to the issue helps us fix things faster.

Billing

How much does Tempo cost?

Tempo will be $7.99 AUD per month when we launch. Payment isn't open yet. Joining the waitlist is free and reserves your spot. See current details on the pricing section.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime. All billing is handled securely through Stripe, so there is no App Store subscription to manage. To cancel or change your plan, email tempo.calendar.ai@gmail.com and we'll take care of it right away.

Privacy & data

How is my data used and protected?

We use your data to run the assistant features you enable, and we don't sell your personal information. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with access controls and regular security reviews. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email tempo.calendar.ai@gmail.com from your account address and we'll process the deletion. We delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep certain records.

Troubleshooting

My calendar or email isn't syncing. What should I do?

First, open the Updates page and confirm the integration is still connected. Reconnecting the service there usually resolves sync issues. For Notion, also check that your allowed pages are still selected in Settings. If it persists, email us with the affected service and roughly when it last worked, and we'll investigate.

I can't sign in or access the app.

Make sure you're using the email address your invite was sent to, and that you've accepted the TestFlight invitation. If you're still stuck, email tempo.calendar.ai@gmail.com and we'll get you in.

Still need a hand?

Our team is small, responsive, and genuinely happy to help. Send us a message and we'll get back to you, usually within one business day.
