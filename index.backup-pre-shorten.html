FAQ

Common questions

Who is Tempo for?

Students and professionals on iPhone who want one calendar app with planning and inbox help built in.

Is Tempo only on iOS?

Yes. Native iPhone app in private beta today. App Store timing will be announced later.

How much does the beta cost?

Waitlist is free. You get 2 weeks of full access, then your first paid month free. After that, $7.99 AUD per month unless we notify you otherwise.

When will I get access?

Usually within 48 hours by email. Check spam if you do not see TestFlight.

Does Tempo send or reschedule without asking?

No. It suggests drafts and calendar changes. You review and approve before anything goes out or moves.

How is my data used?

We use your data to run the assistant features you enable. We do not sell your personal information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How is Tempo different from Motion or Reclaim?

They auto-place blocks in calendars you already use. Tempo is a full native calendar app with inbox drafts and briefings built in.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel a subscription through the App Store at any time. Leaving the waitlist before invite is as simple as not accepting the TestFlight email.
