FAQ

Common questions

What is Tempo?

Tempo is an AI executive assistant with a calendar interface for iPhone. It helps you plan your week, draft replies, and stay on top of email and events in one app.

Which apps does Tempo connect to?

Today we support Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, and Outlook. More integrations are on the way.

Is Tempo only on iOS?

Yes. Tempo is a native iOS app in private beta. We will share App Store plans as we get closer to public launch.

How much does the beta cost?

Join the waitlist for free. Beta members get 2 weeks of full access, then the first subscription month free. After that, plans start at $7.99 AUD per month unless we notify you otherwise.

When will I get access?

We send TestFlight invites within 48 hours of joining the waitlist, depending on capacity. Check your email, including spam.

Does Tempo send emails or move meetings without asking?

Tempo suggests drafts and schedule changes. You review and approve before anything is sent or applied to your calendar.

How is my data used?

We use your data to run the assistant features you enable. We do not sell your personal information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel a subscription through the App Store at any time. Leaving the waitlist before invite is as simple as not accepting the TestFlight email.
