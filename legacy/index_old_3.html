How Tempo works

Four steps from timetable to study plan.

Most students open their course profile too late. Tempo runs a sequence (import, discover, track, ask) that turns your semester into a plan before week one ends.

01 / Import

Your timetable. All your courses.

Paste your UQ ICS timetable URL from the student portal. Tempo detects every enrolled course, every contact hour, and sets up your calendar in seconds. Zero manual entry.

  • Works with UQ's student portal ICS URL
  • Auto-detects course codes and names
  • Updates automatically each semester
02 / Discover

Every assignment. Already there.

Tempo cross-references 1,261 UQ courses and pulls every assessment (name, weight, due date, category, and hurdle requirements) before you've opened a single course profile.

  • 1,261 UQ courses pre-loaded
  • Weights and due dates auto-detected
  • Hurdle requirements flagged
03 / Track

The whole semester, at a glance.

A dedicated timeline tab shows every assessment with urgency badges, teaching week numbers, and category filters. Create a study block for any task in one tap so nothing sneaks up.

  • Urgency badges: overdue, this week, upcoming
  • Filter by course, type, or teaching week
  • One-tap study block creation
04 / Ask

Meet Tempo: your AI agent.

Open the chat and talk to Tempo like a teammate: it already sees your timetable, enrolled courses, weights, deadlines, and hurdles. Ask in plain English. Every reply is grounded in your real week, not generic study tips from the web.

  • "Tempo, what's due for my courses this week?"
  • "Tempo, help me plan MECH2300 around my actual classes."
  • "Tempo, find time for my BIOL report before the deadline."
